The Garden of Consequences — an interactive painting inside LivingRoom

Step inside Hieronymus Bosch's The Garden of Earthly Delights, painted around
1500 and now in Madrid's Prado Museum. Point and pinch to uncover a darker
possibility within the same landscape; release, and the garden returns.

We adapt Bosch's contrast between earthly pleasure and Hell through aligned
panoramic layers, gesture-controlled masks, AI-generated motion, and narration.
Four optional prepared loops explore day, night, and seasons. A separate
realistic Live Lab scenario demonstrates prompt-steered Orbis Dynamic generation.

Our question is simple: can an interaction change how we read a painting?
The prototype explores a method that could be adapted to other artworks through
artwork-specific layouts, paired imagery, and carefully directed motion.
